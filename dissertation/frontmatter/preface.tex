I used generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools during the revision of this dissertation to assist with editing. This included improving the clarity and readability of my writing and helping me identify areas that could be better explained or structured. The fieldwork for this research was conducted by me. I met and worked directly with real participants at the various research sites in Cape Town, where I conducted the interviews, workshops and co-design activities described in this dissertation. The research data, analysis and interpretation presented are based on this fieldwork and are my own work. I reviewed AI-assisted suggestions before using them and remained responsible for the final content. All references included in this dissertation are real academic sources that can be obtained online at their various repositories. AI was used as an editing aid and not as a replacement for my fieldwork, research, analysis or academic judgement.
